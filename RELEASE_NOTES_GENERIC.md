## Winter Arc Generic v1.2.0

A separate, customizable edition for anyone starting a 90-day habit arc.

- Eight neutral, editable starter habits with morning, focus and evening sections.
- No preloaded personal name, company work, religious routine, private goals or history.
- Your arc starts on the first-launch date; change it in Settings.
- Add your own habits, goals and weekly project tasks.
- Daily check-ins, weekly tracking, progress views and local backup/import.
- Native task-linked alarms with ringing, vibration, Snooze and Dismiss; full-screen presentation requires Android permission and depends on device state.
- Dates and alarm schedules follow your phone's time zone.

Download **winter-arc-generic-v1.2.0.apk** below. This debug-signed preview installs as **Winter Arc Generic** (`com.winterarc.tracker`), separately from the personal edition. Your existing personal app and its data are not replaced.

Data is stored locally, with no account or automatic cloud sync. Export a backup before uninstalling. No alarms are enabled until you configure them. Native scheduling and builds were checked; lock-screen ringing and vibration still require testing on a physical Android phone.
